Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 3rd World ("we", "us", "the site") handles information when you visit this website or play the game. We aim to collect as little as possible.

1. Information you give us

To start a game you choose an explorer nickname and a character. You don't need to create an account, and we never ask for your real name, email address or password to play. Please don't use personal information as your nickname.

2. Game progress stored on your device

Your game progress (nickname, character, current world, missions, inventory and play time) and your settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

3. Optional cloud saves

If cloud saving is enabled on this site, your game progress is also stored with our database provider, Supabase, linked to an anonymous identifier created for your browser. It is not linked to your name or email. It is used only to keep your progress and to maintain the game. To request deletion, contact us (see section 9).

4. Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show advertisements, which help keep the game free. Please note:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used, through a consent message shown on the site. You can change your choice at any time from that message.

5. Fonts and hosting

Web fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts are loaded. The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to deliver and secure the website.

6. How information is used

We use the information described above only to run the game, remember your progress, keep the site secure, and show advertising. We do not sell personal information.

7. Children

3rd World is a family-friendly game, but this website is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, and to object to or restrict its processing. Contact us to make a request.

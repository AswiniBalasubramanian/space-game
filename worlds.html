The Worlds

Five places, one journey. Each world has its own light, its own people and its own way of asking for help.

Home

The journey starts in a small wooden house in a wide green meadow. Inside, everything feels lived in: a bookshelf of worn books, family photographs, a kettle on the stove, a hologram clock your father built, and a cat asleep on the rug. In the bedroom, your mother rests beside an old oxygen machine whose reserve is nearly empty.

Outside, the world is peaceful — chickens in the garden, laundry moving in the wind, a well, and the family's little blue car parked on the stone path. High above, a vast pale planet and its rings hang in the daytime sky, a hint of how big the universe really is.

The Space Between

Lifting off from home takes you into a living cosmos painted in deep blues and teals. Comets streak past, asteroids tumble in slow rings around your home planet, distant spiral galaxies glow on the horizon, and three enormous black holes wait in the dark — each wrapped in a golden, swirling accretion disk that bends the light around it.

Fly close and your instruments pick up an unknown world. Choose to enter, and the stars stretch, the screen goes dark and silent… and then a flash of light opens onto somewhere new.

01 · Farm World

An endless agricultural landscape lies beneath a celestial ring so large it arches from one horizon to the other. Floating islands of farmland drift overhead, wheat ripples in the wind, windmills turn, sheep wander, and sunflowers line the irrigation channels.

The mission — Harvest Day: the farmer Hollis needs help bringing in the day's harvest before nightfall. Pick the glowing sun-gourds from the ring-shaped crop fields and carry them to the cart by the barn. In return, he gives you something that has glowed in the soil since the day you arrived.

02 · Knowledge World

A wealthy civilisation built on floating platforms above a sea of clouds. Golden towers, holographic signs and a great glass library full of drifting books shine in the light of a vast black hole overhead. But the library is for members only.

The mission — Share Knowledge: below the bright city, in the shadow of the platforms, live children who have never been taught. Find them, answer their questions about numbers, plants, words and the stars, and light a lantern of understanding for each one.

03 · Hunger World

A dry, cracked land at dusk, gathered around a lake so still that it reflects the entire sky — clouds, stars and the black hole on the horizon. A small fishing village lives on the eastern shore, but its dock is broken and its granary is empty.

The mission — Feed the World: gather fallen branches, driftwood and stones, repair the dock, catch fish, cook them over the village fire, and fill the granary so no one sleeps hungry tonight.

The way home

With all three Oxygen Cores, a blue signal guides you back across space to your home planet. What happens next is best discovered for yourself.
